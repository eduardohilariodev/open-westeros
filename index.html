          Travel time

          Measured along the ground of this planet (radius 6,915 km). Paces are rule-of-thumb estimates for a travel day of 8 hours; the books give few speeds, and the dragon's is an assumption. A league is 3 miles on land and 3 nautical miles at sea.

          Contribute

          Open Westeros is built in the open, by anyone who loves a corner of the Known World.

          1. Open by default. MIT code; pull requests are the plan.
          2. Community built. Each castle, city, road and river is a contribution.
          3. Canon first, sources always. The books, then the wiki. When canon is silent, we say so.
          4. Honest scale. One planet, real metres.
          5. Reproducible, not redistributed. Copyrighted art is never committed.
          6. Small, readable pieces. The simplest thing that works.
          7. Credit the makers. Names stay attached to what they made.
