# Lore conflicts and judgment calls

Where sources disagree, or the evidence supports more than one reading, and what the map does. The order of precedence is in `CLAUDE.md`. Add an entry whenever a check turns up a disagreement; a short quote is enough.

| Question | Sources | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Length of the Kingsroad | wiki Kingsroad: "almost two thousand miles"; blog and the World of Ice & Fire's history: 2,600 miles Storm's End to the Wall | Our road is ~2,980 mi. The blog's road lengths are close to straight-line distances, so no figure is forced on the painted road. Open. |
| Latitude of the Wall | blog, size of Westeros: Wall to the pole 2,086 mi (about 62.0°N); same post: Arctic Circle "exactly 300 miles north of the Wall" (about 62.5°N); blog, latitude post: 61.97°N | 62.0°N (two of three; the data's mean is 62.03°N). `src/config.ts` had said 66.6°N, which is the Arctic Circle. |
| Where the Goldroad crosses the Blackwater | wiki Goldroad: the "Known World" and "The West" maps differ from the "Map of the South" and the "Riverlands" map; the World of Ice & Fire: the plains "south of the Blackwater, near to where the Goldroad would run one day" | Painted route south of the Blackwater and of Stoney Sept (117 mi south), which the source maps and the World of Ice & Fire agree on. |
| Boneway as a road | ASOS, Tyrion: "climb the Boneway, turn east near Summerhall, and come up the kingsroad"; wiki: through the Red Mountains from Yronwood past the Wyl to Summerhall | The painted road goes round the gulf (1.8x winding). A straight link would cross the Sea of Dorne, so the detour stays. |
| Names of the eastern roads | World of Ice & Fire: Steel Road from Vaes Dothrak to Kayakayanaya, Stone Road from Vaes Dothrak to Vaes Jini and Samyriana, Sand Road from Qarth to Tiqui guarded by Bayasabhad; the old data had the "Steel Road" from Tiqui to Kayakayanaya | The book wins; `lore-roads.json` follows it. |
| White Harbor on the White Knife | wiki White Harbor: "on the mouth of the White Knife"; ADWD, Davos: "on the eastern shore where the White Knife plunged into the firth" | East shore of the firth, 6.7 mi from the river's mouth. The book is the more exact. |
| Islands missing from the vector land | Bear Island, the Three Sisters, Claw Isle, Great Wyk, the Shield Islands, and others have castles in the data and no island polygon | Not moved. Listed in `tools/books/island-places.json`; the land polygons need islands, not the places moving. |
| Distance of the Water Gardens from Sunspear | wiki and AFFC: "three leagues of coast road" (9 mi); the data has 14 mi | Within the tolerance (8 mi absolute). Canon gives no direction, so the position is not moved. |
| Eyrie, Gates of the Moon, Giant's Lance | World of Ice & Fire, AGOT (Catelyn, Tyrion), ASOS, AFFC, Fire & Blood, wiki, blog all agree: the Gates stand at the foot of the Giant's Lance, the Eyrie on a shoulder above, a day-long climb past Stone, Snow and Sky. No source gives a distance. The data had them 59-117 mi apart. | Eyrie kept (north edge of the Vale proper); the Lance is 1.5 mi NW and the Gates 4 mi S of it. The Bloody Gate to the Gates is only timed (AGOT: set out within the hour, reached the Gates after dark), about 105 mi on the map; no distance is stated, so it stays. |
